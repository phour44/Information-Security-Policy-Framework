# Security Policy Development Project  
# Phour Global Limited  
Enterprise Security Policies and Standards Portfolio

## Overview
This repository contains a structured collection of enterprise cybersecurity policies and standards developed for **Phour Global Limited**.  
The documents establish governance, risk, and compliance controls across identity, access, encryption, artificial intelligence usage, vulnerability management, and risk communication.

The policies are designed to be practical, enforceable, and audit-ready, aligned with globally recognized security frameworks and regulatory expectations.

## Organization
**Company Name:** Phour Global Limited  
**Domain:** Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance  
**Policy Review Cycle:** Annual or upon major risk or regulatory change

## Objectives
- Define clear rules for access, identity, and privilege management.
- Protect sensitive data using approved cryptographic standards.
- Govern the ethical and lawful use of artificial intelligence.
- Reduce cyber risk through proactive vulnerability management.
- Enable consistent, executive-level cybersecurity risk communication.
- Support audits, compliance reviews, and security assessments.

## Scope
These policies apply to all Phour Global Limited employees, contractors, affiliates, and third parties with access to company systems, applications, or data.

## Policies and Standards Included

### Access Management Policy
Establishes controls for requesting, approving, granting, reviewing, and revoking access to systems and data.

Key focus areas:
- Role-based access control and separation of duties.
- Least privilege enforcement through documented ACLs.
- Regular access reviews and logging of access changes.
- Encryption enforcement for data at rest and in transit through access controls. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

### Identity Management Policy
Defines the lifecycle management of digital identities across the organization.

Key focus areas:
- Centralized identity providers and single sign-on.
- Automated provisioning and deprovisioning via HR systems.
- Strong authentication, MFA, and account lockout controls.
- Identity reviews and user behavior logging. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

### Privileged Account Management Policy
Controls the creation and use of accounts with elevated privileges.

Key focus areas:
- Dedicated privileged accounts with no shared credentials.
- Inventory and monitoring of all privileged accounts.
- Credential rotation using PAM systems.
- MFA and logging for all privileged access activities. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

### Password Construction Standard
Defines technical requirements for strong password creation.

Key focus areas:
- Minimum password length of 16 characters.
- Use of passphrases for improved security and usability.
- Periodic password testing and enforcement actions.
- Coverage of user, system, application, and network accounts. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

### Acceptable Encryption Standard
Specifies approved encryption algorithms and key management practices.

Key focus areas:
- Use of AES for symmetric encryption.
- RSA and ECC for asymmetric encryption.
- Compliance with NIST FIPS 140-2 standards.
- Secure key generation, exchange, authentication, and storage. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

### Artificial Intelligence Acceptable Use Standard
Governs the ethical, lawful, and secure use of artificial intelligence.

Key focus areas:
- AI system approval through an internal AI Committee.
- Prohibition of sensitive data use in open AI systems.
- Mandatory documentation and human review of AI outputs.
- Compliance with global laws and EU AI risk classifications. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

### Technology Equipment Disposal Standard
Defines secure methods for disposing of technology assets.

Key focus areas:
- Secure data wiping and physical destruction of storage media.
- Chain-of-custody and disposal documentation.
- Controlled employee purchase process for retired equipment.
- Environmental and regulatory compliance. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

### Vulnerability Management Policy
Establishes a proactive approach to identifying and remediating security weaknesses.

Key focus areas:
- Continuous vulnerability scanning and tracking.
- CVE- and configuration-based detection.
- Risk-based prioritization and remediation tracking.
- Reporting to technical, business, and executive stakeholders. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

### Risk Communication Management Policy
Defines how cybersecurity risks are reported across the organization.

Key focus areas:
- Use of GRC platforms for centralized risk tracking.
- Dashboard-based reporting by safeguard and business unit.
- Regular risk reporting to executives and stakeholders.
- Support for informed, risk-based decision-making. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}
